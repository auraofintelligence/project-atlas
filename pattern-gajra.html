GAJRA pattern ancestry

The name stayed. The implementation kept changing.

The useful lineage is not “repo A became repo B.” It is the separation between the ideas that persist, the mechanisms that were tried, and the corrections that followed.

2022

Before the current public repos

Joyful Responsible Abundance already appears as a stated motivation alongside an existing personal tool ecology for VR, simulation, 360° capture, machine learning, music and worldbuilding.

2023

GAJRA becomes a values-mapping process

The mission is described as a reliable, evolutionary visualisation of humane values, actions and objectives, inviting people from every region, tribe, language and culture to contribute their own definitions rather than merely consume a finished doctrine.

2023

Human + AI co-development becomes explicit

Aura, GAJRA, Gamify Democracy, artistic world-votes, mixed-reality games and LLM collaboration are explored as one ecosystem for reflection, coordination and alignment.

Persistent invariants

What keeps surviving

  • Joyful Responsible Abundance as the organising compass
  • Human self-reflection before machine alignment
  • Many cultures contributing meaning in their own language and context
  • Art, music, games and festivals as serious coordination media
  • Aura as the personal interface between human meaning and machine capability
  • Local participation connected into larger networks
  • Technology as a tool for increasing legibility, agency and collaboration

Historical experiments

Mechanisms that were tried

  • ICO / token funding
  • DAO governance
  • Blockchain voting at planetary scale
  • Token incentives, staking and NFT-linked participation
  • Central-team-to-token-holder governance transitions
  • A single world-vote concept for common values
  • Live Aid 2025 as a specific synchronising event

These belong in the archive as experiments, not as automatic statements of the current model.

Later corrections

What the braid learned

  • Federation rather than one central global authority
  • Living plurality rather than pretending one values vector resolves difference
  • Local interpretation and locally rooted associations
  • Permission, provenance and the right to withdraw
  • No veto powers or disguised reserved-control mechanisms
  • C-Hour as non-monetary contribution, separated from wages and financial claims
  • Convening and comparison without claiming authority to define the good life for everyone
