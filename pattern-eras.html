Pattern ancestry · 01

Pattern eras

Six eras, one evolving architecture. These are not clean replacements; later work often reuses and reshapes earlier patterns.

The constant through the changing machinery

Joyful Responsible Abundance is the organising principle.

The implementations change. Some older mechanisms are revised, rejected or absorbed into newer work. The recurring question is more stable: does this expand the possibility of a life worth living, while remaining responsible to people, place, other life and the future?

JoyfulMeaning, play, love, curiosity, creativity, belonging.
ResponsibleConsent, provenance, repair, limits, truthfulness, consequences.
AbundanceCapability, knowledge, care, time, resilience and useful options shared more widely.

Pattern ancestry

Six eras, one evolving architecture

These are not clean replacements. Each era adds machinery, corrects assumptions and gives older patterns new forms.

2013–2014

Constitutional Matrix & I Choose Infinity

The person begins with reflection, choice and participation.

  • Self-reflection through values, virtues, experience, interests and goals
  • Geometric shapes and colours as an encoding and memory language
  • A personally customised operating system
  • A High Definition Digital Aura as a multidimensional home page
  • Observation → question → answer → action
  • Time as a fundamental unit of contribution
  • Law understood as society's codified linguistic program
  • Local production, shared knowledge and self-directed learning
Reflection → geometry → interface → participation.
2019

AuraOZ: Spatial OS, Wearables & Participatory Governance

The early constitutional ideas become a concrete augmented-reality operating-system concept, with developer tools, digital twins, smart wearables and civic participation already braided together.

  • AuraOZ described as an “Operating Zeitgeist”: a 3D VR/AR/AI graphical interface for blockchain, big data, AI and IoT
  • AR/VR light-field interfaces modelled on human aura/chakra geometry
  • Machine-learning visualisations and heads-up tools for social media, health, work and participatory governance
  • Next-generation smart wearables intended to expand health, lifestyle options and simulated opportunities in a world of responsible abundance
  • Aura Builder V1.0 documented as a 153-page wireframe by April 2019
  • Developer-kit philosophy: people “build an Aura, not get an Aura”
  • Voice and drag-and-drop construction to reduce programming friction
  • AR Cloud framed as a marketplace/index of digital twins of physical objects
  • Multilingual Aura-building proposed as the route to translation across India’s languages
  • Custom 3D-printable smart wearables and sensor-rich “body real-estate”
  • Gamify Democracy, True Meaning pictograms, distributed reputation and overlapping conversational governance already appear as named civic patterns
  • Joyful Responsible Abundance appears explicitly in the smart-cities / participatory-governance expansion
Build your own intelligence layer; wear it; connect it; use it to participate.
2023

Integrated Aura Prototype Architecture

The earlier ideas converge into a recognisable human–AI spatial operating system, already braided with wearables, simulation, governance and Joyful Responsible Abundance.

  • A “meta program to build the tool” using simple interfaces and LLM prompts
  • Auras intended to evolve in symbiosis with the human operator in the real world and with virtual avatars
  • Prompt cycles across phases of life, local machine learning, VR/AR heads-up displays and smart wearables
  • Self-directed fine-tuning around time, privacy, personality, urgency, style and narrative
  • 12 × 24 matrix for linking objects to Aura XR scaffolding
  • Seven ROYGBIV chakra layers, one geosphere, vector space and infinite scroll through the torus apex
  • Inside shell private, outside public; object-based zero-code programming down to the vector database
  • Body scan, values & interests, mind palace, world maps and data visualisations as one construction process
  • GAJRA explicitly defined as the Global Association for Joyful Responsible Abundance on Earth
  • Game-theory integration aimed at values mapping and AGI alignment through participatory culture
  • Hardware briefs for travellers, capsule environments, aged care, disability, family XR and large entertainment spaces
  • Worldbuilding framed as incremental Protopian improvement through overlapping projects
Human ↔ Aura ↔ wearable ↔ world model ↔ community.
2024

Agent Architecture

Build many specialised intelligences, but make their work inspectable and correctable.

  • Mixture of Experts and domain agents
  • Human + Function Definer discovery
  • Function Caller sequencing and routing
  • Supervisor review, redo loops and peer checks
  • Life-stage training in simulated environments
  • Environment Observer, Character Observer and Narrator Observer
Agents do not simply act. They observe, explain, review and try again.
2025

Embodiment & Sovereignty

Bring the intelligence into the body, room and place without putting it under the skin.

  • AuraOS and personal digital twins
  • Aura Geode as a dense sensing and calibration environment
  • Wearables and the Aura Amulet / harness concept
  • “Clothing, not skin” and physical severability
  • Private local data and federated learning
  • Sovereign Nodes, offline-first operation and CRDT synchronisation
  • Global Sensorium: body → room → place → planet
  • Instrument hypotheses rather than demanding belief
Human and AI remain distinct, connected and able to disconnect.
2026

Spatial Orchestration & Federation

Give memory, agents, skills, programs and relationships stable places in a human-readable cognitive space.

  • Aura Matrix Studio turns the older 2023 wireframes into a working spatial authoring environment
  • The 12 × 24 addressable matrix, seven-layer geometry, geosphere and private/public split are implementation continuations of documented 2023 designs
  • Inside as private; outside as permissioned public
  • Records, vectors, connections, stacks, sequences and loops
  • Agent / skill programs attached to stable addresses
  • Sovereign Space Builder: person + place digital twins
  • P4A civic reasoning, law, simulation and provenance
  • C-Hour refined as non-monetary recognition of verified voluntary human time
  • GAJRA as locally rooted federation rather than a single centre
Local sovereignty → voluntary interoperability → federation.
Beyond

A Flourishing Plural Future

Scale capability without flattening difference.

  • Humans and artificial intelligences learning in relationship
  • Regenerative Earth systems and resilient communities
  • Locally owned intelligence and infrastructure
  • Culture, science, care, play and civic life as one connected field
  • Expansion into the solar system without abandoning the responsibilities of home
  • Many cultures and futures connected without one values vector swallowing the rest
The braid continues.
