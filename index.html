How dates work here

Original work date, not housekeeping date

Loading the audit method...

What counts: the earliest audited commit with substantive project material, recorded in Brisbane local date.

What does not count: a later licence, standard template or small maintenance change that makes GitHub say a project was just updated.

When evidence is missing: the project says so instead of inventing a date.
